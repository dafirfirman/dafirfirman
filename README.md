## 👨‍💻 About Me

Hi, I'm **Firman Damai Zebua**. I build software, teach the next generation of developers, and research how machine learning can make systems more secure.

- 🎓 Master's student in **Informatics Engineering** at **Universitas Sumatera Utara (USU)**
- 🏫 Teacher at **SMK Swasta YAPIM Biru-Biru**, teaching Coding & Artificial Intelligence and Programming Fundamentals
- 🔬 Research focus: **cross-dataset DDoS detection** and **Indonesian NLP**
- 📱 Building mobile apps with **Flutter** and web systems with **Laravel**
- 🤖 Leading hands-on **robotics and networking** activities for vocational students
- 🎯 Long-term goal: become a **university lecturer and researcher**

### 🎓 SIAK: Academic Information System

<br>`Laravel` · `PHP` · `MySQL`
<br>🔗 [View repository](https://github.com/dafirfirman/REPO_NAME)

### 🛡️ Cross-Dataset DDoS Detection
Master's thesis research on why ML models that score near-perfect in-dataset collapse across datasets, and how domain adaptation with under 1% target data recovers performance. Includes the finding that roughly 59% of rows in CICDDoS2019 are duplicates.
<br>`Python` · `Scikit-Learn` · `Random Forest` · `KNN` · `CICDDoS2019`
<br>🔗 [View repository](https://github.com/dafirfirman/REPO_NAME)

### 💬 IndoBERT SMS Spam Classifier
Multi-class Indonesian SMS spam detection using IndoBERT with SHAP-based interpretability.
<br>`Python` · `PyTorch` · `IndoBERT` · `SHAP`
<br>🔗 [View repository](https://github.com/dafirfirman/REPO_NAME)

### 🤖 Line Follower Robot
Educational robotics project (Projek P5) for vocational students, built on Arduino with an 8-channel reflectance sensor array and motor driver.
<br>`Arduino` · `C++` · `Robotics`
<br>🔗 [View repository](https://github.com/dafirfirman/REPO_NAME)
